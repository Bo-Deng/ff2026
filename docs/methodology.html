How the reports
are calculated.

Actual scores and effective lineups

League scoring settings, weekly lineups and final points come from Sleeper.

Scoring trends

Week-over-week changes compare final team totals. Weekly ranks compare the eight teams. All-play records compare each team with all seven opponents each week. A 200-point week or scoring streak describes observed results, not future performance.

Projected finishing scores

At each timestamp: actual points plus the projected points still to come. Future NFL games retain their baseline; remaining projections decline with regulation game-clock time. Sleeper’s projected stats are rescored under this league’s rules. Missing custom defense counters use prior team history, shrunk toward the NFL league average.

Experimental win probabilities

Position-level uncertainty is fitted to prior-week projection errors. The primary model assumes independent Gaussian remaining-score errors, with variance declining with regulation time. It computes the chance one final score exceeds the other.

Shading changes uncertainty by ±20% and tests a chosen same-game correlation scenario. It is an assumption-sensitivity range, not a calibrated confidence interval.

Pivotal-play labels

We compare the post-play probability with a same-clock counterfactual removing that play’s net fantasy scoring. Clock and uncertainty stay fixed, and contributions to both fantasy teams on the same NFL play are combined. These percentage-point effects are not two consecutive historical live readings.

Timeline display

NFL game windows retain proportional real-time spacing. Gaps without any NFL games are shortened to narrow, hatched bands. Labels retain actual Pacific event times. The three largest absolute scoring effects per matchup are annotated.

Limitations

  • Historical projection versions are not verified pre-kickoff archives. Effective lineups are held fixed retrospectively.
  • Injury exits, inactive status, possession and changing usage are not reconstructed.
  • Correlations are not learned, and defense thresholds are not simulated structurally.
  • Gaussian tails can allow implausible negative remaining contributions and distort late-game odds.
  • The model is not calibrated for precise historical live odds; use it for cautious comparisons and candidate pivotal moments.

Sources and attribution

  • Sleeper API: league settings, matchup points, rosters and player metadata. Weekly stats and projections use undocumented endpoints.
  • nflverse / nflfastR: processed play-by-play, with raw NFL game data used when a cleaned game was not yet available. Data attribution: CC BY 4.0.
  • DynastyProcess / ffverse: cross-platform player IDs.

Reports are snapshots and may change after stat corrections. No visitor account, live data collection or backend is required.
