How to read these forecasts

Week 12 optimizes unplayed starting slots from each current roster, respecting all 12 starting slots. Once an NFL game is final, its Sleeper starting slots stay fixed at their earned points with no remaining scoring variance; bench players from completed games cannot enter the lineup. Players marked Out or IR and players on reserve are excluded; questionable players remain eligible. Unavoidable vacancies are filled with generic replacement players, estimated separately each week as the median projection of the top five unrostered players at the needed position. FLEX takes the best eligible replacement baseline. Rostered players are retained wherever they can fill a slot.

Week 12 replacement points: QB 15.5 · RB 8.4 · WR 8.3 · TE 8.3 · K 7.0 · DEF 8.8.

Replacement players carry position-level scoring uncertainty. These are hypothetical fill-ins, not roster moves; competitive waiver access is not modeled.

Season odds simulate Weeks 12–14 on the real schedule, then a six-team bracket in Weeks 15–17. Seeds 1–2 get byes; seed 1 faces the 4/5 winner, and seed 2 the 3/6 winner. Future weeks re-optimize each roster using that week’s projections, allowing projected returns and reserve activations. No future trades or new injuries are modeled.

Player score uncertainty uses position-level projection error from Weeks 1–8. Scores are independent across players and weeks; these are approximate model probabilities, not Sleeper odds. Small differences should not be treated as precise. Forecasts reflect this snapshot and do not refresh automatically.
